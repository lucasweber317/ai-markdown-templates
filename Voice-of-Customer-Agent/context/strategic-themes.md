# Strategic Themes

The strategic directions your product is committed to. The synthesis uses these to note when a customer theme aligns with (or cuts against) existing strategy — not to filter what customers are allowed to say.

## How to Use This File

- 3–5 themes works best; these are strategy-level, not feature-level.
- The agent flags alignment ("this ask supports <Theme 2>") but never suppresses off-strategy signal — off-strategy demand is exactly what strategy reviews need to see.
- Revise when strategy genuinely changes, not per quarter.
