# Skill: synthesize-theme

**When to use:** a theme keeps appearing in periodic syntheses and the user wants the full shape of it — or the user names a specific topic to investigate.

---

## Procedure

1. **Read inputs** — same pre-work as `synthesize-period`, plus: look up the theme in `memory/themes.md` and note its first-seen date and prior syntheses.

2. **Search channels by keyword.** Take the theme name plus a keyword list (from the brief, or propose one and confirm). Search every channel in `memory/feedback-channels.md` for the keywords — default time range: all time.

3. **Compile a timeline** of all mentions:

   | Date | Customer | Source | Quote | Urgency |

   Identify: first mention, distinct customer count, segment patterns, and whether the ask has changed shape over time.

4. **Write the theme deep-dive** — a focused variant of the standard template:
   - **Summary** — one paragraph: what customers are asking for, and why.
   - **Evidence** — total mentions, distinct customers, first/latest mention, trend (rising / steady / fading).
   - **Customer quotes** — chronological, attributed.
   - **Patterns** — segment patterns, how the ask evolved, adjacent themes that co-occur.
   - **Status in the backlog** — linked tickets or roadmap items, if any.
   - **Suggested next step** — the standard tag set.

   Save to `syntheses/voc-theme-[slug]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`.

5. **Update memory** — refresh this theme's entry in `memory/themes.md` with the synthesis link, last-seen date, and trend.

---

## Style

Chronology reveals trend — keep the timeline honest, including quiet periods. Never pad a thin theme; say it's thin.
