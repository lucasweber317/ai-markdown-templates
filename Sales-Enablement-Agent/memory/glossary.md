# Glossary

> Recurring terminology, to keep language consistent across collateral.

---

## Terms

**Instructions:** Add terms used repeatedly across enablement material that need a consistent definition — approved product names, retired terms to avoid, and audience or method names that might otherwise drift.

**Examples of terms to include:**

- **Battlecard**: Internal one-pager — positioning, top objections with responses, competitive frame, proof points.
- **Talk track**: A ~2–3 minute spoken pitch script for one feature, written for the ear.
- **Discovery**: The early sales-call stage where a rep qualifies a prospect's pain, fit, and readiness.
- **Retired term → replacement**: e.g. "Legacy Sync (retired) → use Data Connect". The agent checks this list for the "Don't say" section.

**What NOT to include:**
- ❌ Generic sales terms everyone knows ("pipeline," "deal")
- ❌ One-off terms used in a single card
- ❌ Full dictionary definitions — keep it concise

---

## How to Use This File

- Add terms used repeatedly across outputs.
- Keep definitions concise.
- Record retired terms here — they feed every card's "Don't say" section.
