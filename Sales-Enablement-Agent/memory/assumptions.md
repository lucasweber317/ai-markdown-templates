## Active Assumptions

**Instructions:** Add assumptions here that will shape how multiple pieces of collateral are written over time. Each should be specific enough to guide framing but general enough to remain relevant for months.

**Examples of good assumptions:**
- Mid-market prospects buy on time-to-value; enterprise prospects buy on risk reduction — lead battlecards accordingly
- Reps skim: anything below the objection table is reference material, not call prep
- Existing customers respond better to "turn it on" framing than to re-selling the problem
- Compliance-driven segments need the audit angle in the first three sentences or they disengage

**Examples of assumptions to avoid:**
- ❌ "The Q3 launch webinar is on the 14th" (timing, not an assumption)
- ❌ "Sales wants better materials" (too vague, not actionable)
- ❌ "One rep prefers shorter cards" (specific to one person, not durable)
