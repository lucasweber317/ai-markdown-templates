# Decisions

> Confirmed positioning and packaging decisions that gate what collateral may claim.
> Only record decisions that have been agreed upon.

---

## Confirmed Decisions

**Instructions:** This file is the claim gate — the agent will not state a delivery date, price point, or proof point that isn't recorded here or in `context/roadmap.md`. Capture what was decided, why, and enough context to understand it later.

**Good decision format:**
```
**[Decision Title]**: [What was decided]. [Why this choice over alternatives]. [Any constraints or conditions]. [Date if relevant].
```

**Examples of decisions to capture:**
- **Pricing framing**: The new module is sold as an add-on, not bundled. Collateral answers "is this included?" with the add-on framing.
- **Reference customers**: Two customers approved as named references for this feature; all others are described by segment only.
- **Launch timing language**: Public language is "second half of the year" — no month or quarter until General Availability is confirmed.
- **Competitive stance**: We do not name competitors in customer-facing material; internal cards use the coverage-matrix format.

**Examples of what NOT to capture:**
- ❌ Draft positioning still under discussion
- ❌ One-off wording choices for a single card
- ❌ Anything a future reader couldn't act on

---

## How to Use This File

- Record only confirmed decisions.
- Include enough context to understand the decision later.
- Avoid speculative or tentative entries.
- Do not remove historical decisions without explicit approval.
