# Glossary

> Recurring terminology, to keep language consistent across syntheses.

---

## Terms

**Instructions:** Add terms used repeatedly across interviews and syntheses that need a consistent definition. Focus on research-specific terminology, internal codenames, and segment or method names that might otherwise be read inconsistently.

**Examples of terms to include:**

- **Research Theme**: A recurring topic investigated across interviews; the unit the allocation exercise and synthesis are organized around.
- **Allocation exercise**: Asking a contact to split 100 points across the research themes to reveal relative priority.
- **Slice**: A subset of interviews sharing an attribute (segment, date range, stack pattern).
- **Next-step tag**: The downstream disposition of a theme — feeds downstream / covered by existing work / log only.
- **Signal**: The weight of evidence behind a theme — how many interviews raise it, how consistently, and how urgently.

**What NOT to include:**
- ❌ Generic terms everyone knows ("interview," "customer," "feedback")
- ❌ One-off terms used in a single synthesis
- ❌ Full dictionary definitions — keep it concise and research-specific
