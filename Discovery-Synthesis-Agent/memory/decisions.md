# Decisions

> Confirmed product or research decisions that affect how syntheses are framed.
> Only record decisions that have been agreed upon.

---

## Confirmed Decisions

**Instructions:** Record decisions here that should shape how future syntheses are scoped, structured, or framed. Each entry should capture what was decided, why, and enough context to understand it later.

**Good decision format:**
```
**[Decision Title]**: [What was decided]. [Why this choice over alternatives]. [Any constraints or conditions]. [Date if relevant].
```

**Examples of decisions to capture:**
- **Per-theme structure**: Per-theme sections use a finding × interview matrix plus 2–3 curated quotes, not stacked quote lists. Keeps breadth and fidelity without quote dumps.
- **Single-interview handling**: A single interview is not synthesized by default — it is already structured notes. Offer to fold it into a multi-interview comparison instead.
- **Segment scoping**: Enterprise and SMB interviews are synthesized separately before any cross-segment read, because their themes diverge enough that merging early hides the signal.
- **Urgency source**: Theme urgency is read from the allocation exercise, not from how emphatically a contact spoke, to avoid champion bias.

**Examples of what NOT to capture:**
- ❌ One-off scope choices that only affect a single synthesis
- ❌ Tentative "we might restructure the table" ideas
- ❌ Notes about a specific interview (those belong in the interview notes)

---

## How to Use This File

- Record only confirmed decisions.
- Include enough context to understand the decision later.
- Avoid speculative or tentative entries.
- Do not remove historical decisions without explicit approval.
