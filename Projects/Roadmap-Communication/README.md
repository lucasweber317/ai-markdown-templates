# Roadmap Communication — AI Project Template

Part of the Tactical Product Management AI Workflow Suite  
https://www.tacticalproductmanagement.com

Turns your product roadmap into audience-specific communications: a strategic one-pager for executives, a scannable enablement doc for sales, and a benefits-first roadmap presentation for customers — each shaped to what that audience actually cares about. Works in any AI tool that supports projects — no technical tools needed.

---

## Why

One roadmap, three very different readers. Executives want strategic framing, sales wants talking points they can paste into slides, and customers want benefits with honest timelines. Retelling the same roadmap three ways by hand is slow and drifts out of sync — and it's where accidental promises get made. This template keeps every version consistent, audience-appropriate, and disciplined about commitments.

---

## Setup (5 minutes)

1. In your AI tool, create a **project** (or gem, or assistant) and name it "Roadmap Communication."
2. Copy all of `INSTRUCTIONS.md` into the project's **instructions**.
3. Fill in `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` (your product, strategic themes, quarterly roadmap, and audiences) and upload it to the project as a file.
4. Start a chat and name a roadmap slice and an audience — that's the whole workflow.

---

## Example prompts

- "Create an executive one-pager for the Q2 roadmap."
- "Turn Q2 and Q3 into a sales enablement doc."
- "Northwind asked for a roadmap preview — draft the customer-facing version for Q2."
- "The board wants the full-year view. One page."
- "The Contoso integration work slipped to Q3 — update the sales doc accordingly."

---

## What you'll get

Output shaped to the audience: executives get strategic context, an initiative-to-business-impact table, and a timeline overview on one page; sales gets a quick-reference table (capability, customer value, sales angle), competitive angles, per-persona talking points, and an FAQ — built to paste into slides; customers get a disclaimer-first presentation with a plain "what it does for you" table and honest, quarter-level timing. Copy it into your deck or doc tool of choice.

---

## Tips

- The output is only as good as the roadmap it reads — keep the roadmap section of `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` current, and re-upload it when quarters shift.
- The agent uses quarters instead of dates and says "we plan to" instead of "will launch." That caution is deliberate — only override it for explicitly confirmed commitments.
- When a communication settles something durable — a timeline rule, a positioning choice, a standing disclaimer — add it to the *Decisions & notes* section of `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` and re-upload it.
