## Audiences

One block per audience you communicate the roadmap to. The three below are the standard set; adjust to your organization.

### Executives / board
- **What they care about:** strategic alignment, market opportunity, ROI, risk.
- **Detail level:** one page, business language, quarters not dates.

### Sales / customer success
- **What they care about:** deal-closing capabilities, competitive angles, when they can sell it.
- **Detail level:** scannable tables and one-liners they can paste into slides.

### Customers
- **Who they are:** typical roles, technical level, what they're measured on.
- **What they care about:** what's coming, why it matters to them, honest timing, how to give feedback.

## Decisions & notes

Your running memory. When the agent flags something durable — a confirmed decision, a working assumption, a recurring pattern — add it here and re-upload this file. Remove entries that turn out to be wrong.

- (e.g.) **Decision:** External materials use quarters only (Q2 2027) — never month-level dates, even when engineering has one.
- (e.g.) **Assumption:** Sales needs 30 days of lead time before a launch to prepare customer conversations — flag anything landing closer than that.
