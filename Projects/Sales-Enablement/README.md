# Sales Enablement — AI Project Template

Part of the Tactical Product Management AI Workflow Suite  
https://www.tacticalproductmanagement.com

Turns your product context into sales- and Customer Success–facing collateral when a feature launches: one-page battlecards, short talk tracks a rep can actually speak on a call, and qualifying questions for discovery. Works in any AI tool that supports projects — no technical tools needed.

---

## Why

Enablement material usually goes wrong in one of two ways: it's written feature-first instead of pain-first, or it's full of claims nobody can back. This template enforces the discipline — grounded in your product, personas, and confirmed decisions, with real customer language where you have it and an explicit gap flag where you don't.

---

## Setup (5 minutes)

1. In your AI tool, create a **project** (or gem, or assistant) and name it "Sales Enablement."
2. Copy all of `INSTRUCTIONS.md` into the project's **instructions**.
3. Fill in `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` (your product, personas, roadmap, and confirmed decisions) and upload it to the project as a file.
4. Ask for a piece of collateral — name the feature, the audience, and whether the call is with a new prospect or an existing customer.

---

## Example prompts

- "Create an Account Executive battlecard for Guided Onboarding."
- "Give me a talk track on Guided Onboarding for an expansion call with an existing customer."
- "Qualifying questions for Guided Onboarding — mid-market prospect, first call."
- "Here's a customer research write-up — pull the pain quotes into the battlecard."
- "Rewrite this battlecard for Customer Success Managers."

---

## What you'll get

A finished, shippable piece in chat, ready to copy: a one-page battlecard (positioning, customer pain with quotes, top objections with responses, competitive frame, proof points, and a "Don't say" list), a ~2–3 minute talk track (hook → problem → solution → evidence → close, plus objection responses), or 5–10 grouped discovery questions with answer cues, red flags, and a next-step menu.

---

## Tips

- The agent won't state dates, prices, or proof points that aren't in your context file — that's the point. Record confirmed decisions there and the collateral gets sharper.
- Paste customer research (interview notes, feedback syntheses) into the chat — real quotes beat invented ones, and the agent refuses to invent them.
- When the agent flags a durable positioning decision or assumption, add it to the *Decisions & notes* section of `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` and re-upload it.
