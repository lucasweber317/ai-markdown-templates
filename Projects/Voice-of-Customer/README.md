# Voice of Customer — AI Project Template

Part of the Tactical Product Management AI Workflow Suite  
https://www.tacticalproductmanagement.com

Turns scattered customer feedback — tickets, chat threads, emails, survey results, business-review notes — into themed, evidence-backed reports: what customers are saying, how often, how urgently, and what to do with each signal. Works in any AI tool that supports projects — no technical tools needed.

---

## Why

Each feedback item alone is an anecdote. Read together and tracked over time, they become themes with weight: how many customers, how often, how urgent, and whether the same ask keeps coming back without resolution. This template makes that read disciplined — every claim backed by an attributed verbatim quote, every empty source named instead of papered over.

---

## Setup (5 minutes)

1. In your AI tool, create a **project** (or gem, or assistant) and name it "Voice of Customer."
2. Copy all of `INSTRUCTIONS.md` into the project's **instructions**.
3. Fill in `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` (your product, personas, strategic themes, and feedback channels) and upload it to the project as a file.
4. Add feedback: upload exports (tickets, surveys, transcripts) as files, or paste threads straight into the chat.

---

## Example prompts

- "Here's our Q1 ticket export — what are customers saying? Top themes."
- "Deep-dive the onboarding theme across everything I've uploaded."
- "Business review prep for Northwind — roll up everything we know about them."
- "Churn-risk review for Contoso."
- "Synthesize just the two threads I'm about to paste — nothing else."

---

## What you'll get

An executive summary table ranking themes by frequency × urgency, then one section per theme — a short description, evidence counts (mentions and distinct customers), 2–3 attributed verbatim quotes, an urgency signal, and a next-step tag (*feeds prioritization as new brief* / *covered by existing work* / *log only*) — ending with a sources list that explicitly names any source that returned nothing. Copy it into your wiki or doc tool of choice.

---

## Tips

- Paste or upload the raw feedback, not your summary of it — the agent needs the customers' verbatim words to quote and attribute.
- Give every ask a scope — a period, a theme, a customer, or specific sources. The agent asks rather than guesses.
- When a synthesis surfaces something durable — a recurring theme, a commitment made to a customer — add it to the *Decisions & notes* section of `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` and re-upload it. That's how recurrence flags survive between conversations.
