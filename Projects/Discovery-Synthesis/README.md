# Discovery Synthesis — AI Project Template

Part of the Tactical Product Management AI Workflow Suite  
https://www.tacticalproductmanagement.com

Turns a set of customer interview notes into an evidence-based synthesis: a comparison table, the recurring themes, and verbatim quotes backing every claim. Works in any AI tool that supports projects — no technical tools needed.

---

## Why

A single interview is already structured notes. The value shows up when you read many together: which theme recurs, where accounts diverge, what the pattern is. This template makes that cross-interview read disciplined and repeatable — and stops the AI from paraphrasing your customers or inventing signal.

---

## Setup (5 minutes)

1. In your AI tool, create a **project** (or gem, or assistant) and name it "Discovery Synthesis."
2. Copy all of `INSTRUCTIONS.md` into the project's **instructions**.
3. Fill in `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` (your product, personas, and the 3–6 themes you're researching) and upload it to the project as a file.
4. Add interviews: upload interview notes as files, or just paste a transcript into the chat — the agent structures raw notes for you first.

---

## Example prompts

- "Here are my notes from today's call with [customer] — structure them as an interview note."
- "Synthesize the onboarding theme across all the interviews I've uploaded."
- "Compare the three enterprise interviews."
- "Synthesize the Northwind, Contoso, and Fabrikam interviews only."

---

## What you'll get

An executive summary, a comparison table across interviews, and one section per theme — each with a one-line signal, a finding-by-interview matrix, 2–3 verbatim quotes, and a next-step tag (*feeds prioritization* / *covered by existing work* / *log only*). Copy it into your wiki or doc tool of choice.

---

## Tips

- One interview isn't a synthesis — the agent will say so and offer to hold it for comparison. That's by design.
- The comparison matrix depends on consistent interview structure; let the agent normalize pasted notes rather than skipping that step.
- When a synthesis surfaces a durable decision or assumption, add it to the *Decisions & notes* section of `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` and re-upload it.
